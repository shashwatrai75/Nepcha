CORE VALUES

Why Choose Nepcha?

Altitude-Grown Flavor

Teas cultivated at 1,000–2,000 meters in the Himalayas develop floral, fruity, and delicate notes with remarkable complexity and naturally high antioxidants.

Health Benefits

Rich in catechins, flavonoids, and polyphenols, Nepali teas support heart health, digestion, immunity, mental clarity, weight management, and radiant skin.

Ethical & Sustainable

Nepcha partners directly with Nepali cooperatives for fair wages, organic farming, and regenerative practices—protecting nature and empowering local communities.
